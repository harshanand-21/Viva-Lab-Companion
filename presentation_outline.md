# Short Presentation Outline (about 3 minutes)

## 1. Project
AI-Powered Engineering Lab Companion with Agentic Viva Coach

- A small mini-demo for helping students review an approved lab handout
- No paid model API or key is required
- Current demo content is a fictional Ohm's law guide

## 2. How it works
- A scikit-learn classifier predicts the request type
- A transparent deterministic router selects a local tool
- TF-IDF retrieves relevant handout text; viva questions use fixed templates

## 3. Live demonstration
- Ask: “What is the aim of the experiment?” Show the retrieved section.
- Ask: “Calculate current from 5 V and 100 ohms.” Show 0.05 A.
- Generate three to five viva questions about Ohm's law.

## 4. Scope and safety
- This demonstrates selected ideas from Days 1, 3, 4, and 5 of the program.
- Deep learning, LLM generation, and multi-agent coordination are not implemented.
- The app does not approve experiments; use instructor-approved material and guidance.
